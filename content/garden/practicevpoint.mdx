## "You seem to adopt a practice more often than you just adopt the point" 

D. Zirkle levied that at me during a mentoring session, when I was fighting myself to construct the main message of _Extreme Ownership_. We often find ourselves having differences when discussing philosophy, but I learn a lot.
In this case, we landed on a fundamental difference in the ways we think. He has no problem ascertaining the deeper meaning and five levels of implications from what he reads, whereas sometimes, I need to try out a framework like a costume to understand its value and tradeoffs.
