learned: While the Leitner Box method and the SM-2 algorithm are well-documented, combining them and adding a novel topic score turned out to be a new problem of a different magnitude. Balancing each algorithm's relative influence over a single daily practice queue is significantly more challenging than implementing each separately. To be honest, I'm not satisfied with how the first version resolved that balance. A working mental model is just the beginning, and the implementation will come up with questions that my intuition can't always answer based on that model.
reflection: "The idea is to build corridors of context, not just isolated recall."
